## History
The chart shows each pot's soil water over time. Switch between moisture and
temperature (and °C or °F) above the chart, and choose 6 hours to 90 days
below it. The 7, 30 and 90 day ranges are grayed out until the planter has
been recording longer than the range before each one.
- **A flat dashed line** in a pot's color is the point that pot is watered at.
  When its line drops to it, the pot is due.
- **Dashed vertical lines** are waterings that reached the soil.
- **Amber vertical lines** are waterings that never showed up on the probe.

A gap in a line means that probe couldn't be read at the time.

Tap a pot's name under the chart to hide its line, and tap it again to bring
it back. A hidden pot's name is grayed out.

**Hold to clear history** (five seconds) deletes every reading and watering
the chart draws. Waterings from the last cooldown window stay, because the
planter uses them to avoid watering a pot twice.

### If something looks wrong
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "No readings in this range yet" | A new planter needs a few minutes before the chart fills in. |
| Amber lines | See [Checking each watering](#checking-each-watering). |
