## Calibration
Settings you set once. Most never need changing.

### System Time
The lights follow the planter's own clock. If the time shown isn't your local
time, press **Sync to Local Time**.

### Network name
The address you type to open the planter, such as herman.local. Only change it
if you have more than one planter at home. After renaming, use the new address.

### Limit / home switch
Tests the switch the arm touches when it homes. Press **Watch for 20s**, then
press the switch with your finger. The reading should change while you hold it.

### Pump
Measures how much water the pump moves, so each watering is the right amount.
1. Put the nozzle over a cup on a kitchen scale.
2. Press **Run calibration**. The pump runs for 60 seconds.
3. Run it once to fill the tube, and throw that water away.
4. Run it again, and enter the grams of water in the cup. One gram is one
   milliliter.
5. Press **Save flow rate**.

### Moisture probes
Records each probe's reading in open air, which is its "completely dry" point.
Take all four probes out of the soil, wipe them clean and dry, and press
**Calibrate dry (air)**. A probe that reads very differently from the others may
be faulty.

### LED strip
Choose your strip type first. Then use the **Red**, **Green** and **Blue**
buttons. If the color on the strip doesn't match the button, change the color
order until it does.

### System Values
**Restore to Defaults** puts every setting back to how the planter came. Your
plant profiles, soil profiles and history are kept.

### Software and Recent Logs
Version details and a record of what the planter has done recently. You'll only
need these if support asks for them.

### If something looks wrong
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The home switch reading never changes | The switch or its cable may be loose. Contact support. |
| "The clock has not reached a time server yet" | The planter isn't online yet. Check its Wi-Fi. |
| A pot shows "no usable scale" | That pot's soil profile has no field capacity. See [Soil Profile Editor](#soil-profile-editor). |
