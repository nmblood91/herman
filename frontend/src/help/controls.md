## Controls

### Gantry
The [gantry](#gantry-arm) is the arm that carries the nozzle along the rail.
Press **Home Gantry** first: the arm moves to the end of the rail to find out
where it is. Until it has [homed](#homing), the move buttons stay greyed out.

The move buttons shift the arm 10 or 50 mm at a time, or send it to either end
of the rail.

### Dances
Short routines that move the arm for fun or for testing. The arm homes first if
it needs to, and comes back to the start afterwards.

### Move to Plant and Water Plant
**Move to Plant** sends the arm over a pot without watering. **Water Plant**
moves to the pot and gives it its usual amount of water. A watering takes about
a minute, and the bar at the top of the screen shows how it's going.

### Lighting
- **Zone Schedule:** each pot's lights follow its own hours, in the colour set
  on its card.
- **Manual:** the lights stay on in one colour and brightness. Choose
  **All zones** for the whole strip, or **Separate** to give each pot its own.
- **Rainbow:** a moving rainbow, with its own speed setting. Speed 0 holds it
  still.
- **Off:** all lights off.

### Quiet
[Snooze](#snooze) stops automatic watering for 1, 2 or 4 hours, for example
while you have guests. **Resume watering now** ends it early.

### Power
Always shut down before unplugging the planter. Pulling the plug while it's
running can damage its memory card. Press and keep holding
**Hold to shut down** for a few seconds, so it can't happen by accident.

### If something looks wrong
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The move buttons are greyed out | Press **Home Gantry** first. |
| "The motion board is not responding" | The arm's control board can't be reached. Switch the planter off and on again. If that doesn't help, contact support. |
| The lights don't come on in Zone Schedule | Check the time on the Calibration tab, and the light hours in that pot's plant profile. |
| The lights are the wrong colours | Set the colour order on the Calibration tab. |
