## Controls

### Gantry
The [gantry](#gantry-arm) is the arm that carries the watering nozzle along the
rail from plant to plant. It has to be [homed](#homing) before it can move:
press **Home Gantry**, and the arm moves to the end of the rail to establish
position. Once it has homed, the planter can water on its own.

After homing you can also move the arm yourself. The move buttons shift it 10
or 50 mm at a time, or send it to either end of the rail.

### Dances
Short routines that move the nozzle for fun or for testing. The arm homes first
if it needs to, and comes back to the start afterwards.

### Move to Plant and Water Plant
**Move to Plant** sends the arm over a pot without watering. **Water Plant**
moves to the pot and gives it the watering volume set in its plant profile.

### Lighting
- **Zone Schedule:** each pot's lights follow its own hours, in the color set
  on its card.
- **Manual:** the lights stay on in one color and brightness. Choose
  **All zones** to set the whole strip, or **Separate** to give each pot its
  own color and brightness.
- **Rainbow:** a moving rainbow, with its own speed setting. Speed 0 holds it
  still.
- **Off:** all lights off.

### Quiet
[Snooze](#snooze) stops automatic watering for 1, 2 or 4 hours, for times when
the pump's noise would bother you. **Resume watering now** ends it early.

### Power
Always shut down before unplugging the planter. Pulling the plug while it's
running can damage its memory card. Press and keep holding
**Hold to shut down** for a few seconds, so it can't happen by accident.

### If something looks wrong
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The move buttons are grayed out | Press **Home Gantry** first. |
| "The motion board is not responding" | The arm's control board can't be reached. Switch the planter off and on again. If that doesn't help, contact support. |
| The lights don't come on in Zone Schedule | Check the **System Time** on the Calibration tab, and the light hours in that pot's plant profile. |
| The lights are the wrong colors | Set the color order on the Calibration tab. |
