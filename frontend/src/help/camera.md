## Camera

The live picture from the planter's camera, an optional USB webcam. It turns
on when you open this tab and switches off shortly after you leave it.
Nothing is recorded, and the picture never leaves your home network.

### If something looks wrong
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "No USB camera found" | Check the camera is plugged into the Pi, then press **Try again**. |
| "ffmpeg is not installed" | Run the installer again with the camera plugged in. |
| The picture stopped | Press **Reconnect**. If it stops again, unplug the camera and plug it back in. |
