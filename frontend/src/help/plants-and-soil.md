## Plants and Soil

### Active plants
Each card is one pot. The number on it is how wet the soil is right now, read
from that pot's [moisture probe](#moisture-probe). Tap a card to open its
settings.

**Plant Profile.** What the plant needs: when to water, how much, and its light
hours. Load one from the list to set them all at once. Loading a profile also
switches the pot to the soil the plant prefers.

**Lights.** The colour this pot's lights show during its light hours.
Brightness and hours come from the plant profile.

**Soil Profile.** Which soil mix is in this pot. The planter needs this to make
sense of the probe's reading. If the plant prefers a different mix, the card
says so.

**Watering.**
- **Fixed Position** waters in one spot. It suits small pots.
- **Sweep Range** moves the nozzle back and forth between a left and a right
  edge while it waters. Use it for wide pots, so the water spreads across the
  soil.

To set a position, move the arm over the pot with the buttons on the card, then
press **Use current position**. Each field shows the range it accepts, which
keeps a pot from being watered over its neighbour.

### Plant Profile Editor
Where you make or change plant profiles. A profile holds:
- **Water at:** how dry the soil gets before the plant is watered. The name
  beside it, such as Medium, describes that level.
- **Watering volume:** how much water each watering gives.
- **Light start, hours and brightness:** when that pot's lights come on, for how
  long, and how bright.
- **Notes:** anything you want to remember about the plant.

Changing a profile doesn't change pots that already loaded it. Load it onto the
pot again to update it.

### Soil Profile Editor
Each soil profile describes one soil mix. The two numbers that matter most are:
- **[Field capacity](#field-capacity):** the probe's reading when the soil is as
  wet as it can hold.
- **[Wilting point](#wilting-point):** the reading when the soil is as dry as a
  plant can manage.

The planter reads moisture as 100% at field capacity and 0% at wilting point.

The built-in mixes come with a field capacity already filled in. To measure your
own, water a pot of that mix until water runs out the bottom. Leave it for 24
hours, then choose that pot's probe and press **Measure field capacity**.
Measure again after repotting, because soil holds less water as it ages.

Wilting point is optional. If you leave it blank, the planter estimates it.

### Back up profiles
**Download backup** saves all your plant and soil profiles to a file on your
phone or computer. **Restore from file** brings them back. Do this before any
big change to the planter.

### If something looks wrong
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "No plant profile loaded" | Load one from the list on the card. |
| "No soil profile set" | Choose the mix that's in the pot. |
| The moisture reading shows a dash | The probe isn't being read. Check its cable is plugged in at both ends. |
| The moisture reading never changes | Check the probe is pushed into the soil and not touching the pot's side. |
| A position won't save | It's outside the range shown in the field's name. Move the arm over the right pot and try again. |
